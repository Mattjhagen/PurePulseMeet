# PurePulse Partner Hub - TestFlight & Beta Store Description

## 🚀 Beta App Description (TestFlight & Google Play Beta)

Welcome to the **PurePulse Partner Hub** beta test program! PurePulse Partner Hub is an all-in-one mobile command center designed for PurePulse affiliates, growth partners, and web sales leaders.

This beta release unifies live team coaching huddles, Slack-style group channels, gamified affiliate banking, instant Stripe payouts, and custom social campaign marketing generators into a single cross-platform mobile application.

---

## 🧪 What to Test (TestFlight "What to Test" Notes)

Please focus your testing on the following key feature areas and provide feedback on performance, UI responsiveness, and real-time syncing:

### 1. Live Coaching Huddles & Voice Rooms (`Huddles` Tab)
- Join live Jitsi Meet video and audio huddles directly inside the app.
- Test in-meeting controls: Mute/Unmute microphone, Camera toggle, Raise Hand, and Leave Huddle.
- Verify participant counter and room category pills.

### 2. Community Channels & Strategy Forum (`Channels` Tab)
- Switch between group channels (`#wins-and-success`, `#general`, `#coaching-deals`, `#announcements`).
- Test sending real-time messages, liking posts, and navigating between Direct Messages and the Strategy Forum.

### 3. DoorDash-Style Partner Banking & Cashouts (`Banking` Tab)
- Review the interactive **Virtual Partner Debit Card** with tier status (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, PurePulse Black Card).
- Test opening the **Stripe Connect Cashout Modal** and initiating instant cashouts or standard direct deposit transfers.
- Verify financial balance counters: Available Balance, Accruing MRR, Pending Commissions, and Lifetime Paid Out.

### 4. Referral Links & Campaign Studio (`Overview`, `Studio`, `Flyers` Tabs)
- Test 1-tap copy for your unique referral link (`https://login.purepulse.one/ref/...`) and partner code.
- Generate custom social graphics in **1:1 Square**, **9:16 Story**, and **16:9 Banner** dimensions.
- Copy pre-written outreach scripts and preview full-page Letter 8.5x11 printable flyers.
