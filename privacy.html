Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

1. Overview

PurePulse Inc. ("PurePulse", "we", "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how PurePulse Partner Hub collects, uses, and safeguards personal data when you log in, participate in huddles, manage payouts, or share referral links.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account & Auth Data: Email address, full name, and avatar URL provided via Google OAuth or Apple Sign-In authentication.
  • Financial Payout Information: Stripe Connect Express account tokens, payout transactions, and recurring MRR stats.
  • Community & Video Activity: Messages sent in strategy channels, forum posts, and attendance state during live coaching huddles.

3. How We Use Information

We process your data strictly to deliver application features, authenticate partner sessions, process Stripe commission payouts, and notify you of upcoming team coaching huddles.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell your personal information. Data is processed through secure service providers including Supabase Auth, Stripe Connect, and Expo Push Notifications.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, contact us at support@purepulse.one.
